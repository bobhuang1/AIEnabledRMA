# Unit was dropped or suffered impact damage

## What counts as impact damage

- A visible crack in the housing, screen, or window.
- A unit that flexes, creaks, or has a seam that no longer sits flush.
- A button or key that is stuck or sits proud after an impact.
- A unit that will not power on immediately after a fall, having charged correctly before.

## Why this article is short

Impact damage is a hardware fault. No configuration change, reset, or pairing step will
change the outcome, so there is nothing for the customer to try.

## Evidence to record

- What the customer reports happened, in their own words.
- Which parts are visibly damaged.
- Whether the unit powered on before the incident and does not now, or never worked.

## Important limitation

Impact damage is frequently outside warranty coverage even when the unit is otherwise within
its warranty period. The returns system applies that rule, not this article. Recording the
incident accurately is what allows the correct coverage decision to be made, and
mis-describing an impact as an electronic fault does not help the customer.
