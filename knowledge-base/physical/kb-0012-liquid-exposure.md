# Liquid exposure

## Assess the exposure

- Any liquid ingress, however brief, counts as liquid damage.
- A unit that works normally after exposure is still not sealed against it. Corrosion can
  take weeks to appear and then causes intermittent faults that are hard to attribute.
- Salt water, cleaning agents, and other liquids other than fresh water are more damaging
  than plain water.

## Do not do this

- Do not dry the unit with a heat source. Heat drives moisture into the board and makes
  the damage worse.
- Do not place the unit in rice or any desiccant. It does nothing useful and can introduce
  debris into the ports.
- Do not power the unit on after exposure to see whether it works.

## What to do instead

1. Power the unit off if it is on.
2. Do not charge the unit.
3. Record that liquid exposure occurred, what the liquid was, and approximately when.

## Coverage

Liquid damage is outside warranty coverage. The returns system applies that rule. This
article exists so the customer is not sent through an electronic-fault checklist that
cannot help, and so the exposure is documented before the unit is returned.
