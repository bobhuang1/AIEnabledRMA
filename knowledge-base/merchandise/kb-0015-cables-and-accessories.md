# Cables, adapters, and other non-electronic merchandise

Cables, adapters, mounts, holsters, and straps do not have firmware, batteries, or
radios. The troubleshooting below is the complete applicable set.

## Cable

1. Test the cable with a different device and a different known-good cable. A cable that
   fails with every combination is a fault.
2. Inspect both connector ends for bent pins, corrosion, or a sheath that is cracked right
   at the strain relief. Damage at the strain relief is the most common cable fault and is
   visible on close inspection.
3. Confirm the length and gauge are appropriate for the load. An underspecified cable will
   present as intermittent disconnection at one end only.

## Power adapter

1. Confirm the output rating matches what the connected device requires.
2. Measure the output with a meter if the adapter is suspected. An adapter that no longer
   delivers its rated output fails without any visible sign.
3. A warm adapter is normal. A hot or discoloured adapter is a fault and should not be used.

## Mount and holster

Physical damage only. Check the retention mechanism actually holds the unit, and that the
mount has not cracked around its fixings.

## What is not covered

Installation, configuration, and compatibility advice are not troubleshooting steps and are
not provided here.
