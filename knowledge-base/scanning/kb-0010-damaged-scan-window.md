# Laser or imager window damaged

Physical damage to the scan window is a hardware fault and is not repaired by any
troubleshooting step. This article exists so the customer is not sent through a
decoding checklist that cannot help.

## Confirm the damage

- A visible crack in the window, even a hairline one, is a fault.
- A deeply scratched window that produces a persistent vertical or horizontal artefact in
  the decoded image is a fault.
- A window with a chip at a corner is a fault.

## What a damaged window causes

- Persistent artefact lines through every decoded image.
- Intermittent decode failures that no amount of cleaning resolves, because the artefact
  is in the optical path.
- On laser-based units, a visible beam discontinuity.

## Do not do this

- Do not attempt to polish or buff the window. It removes the optical coating and makes
  decoding worse.
- Do not apply tape or film over the window.
- Do not continue troubleshooting decoding after the damage is confirmed.

## Recording the result

Describe the damage and its location. Physical damage is handled by the returns policy as a
distinct case from electronic faults, and the description is what routes it correctly.
