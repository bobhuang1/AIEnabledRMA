# Reading the charge indicator light

The indicator light on the top face of the unit reports charging state. Check it before
anything else, because most "it will not turn on" reports are actually a flat battery.

| Light pattern | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solid amber | Charging normally | Leave it on the cradle until it goes solid green. |
| Blinking amber | Battery is low but healthy | Continue charging. No action needed. |
| Solid green | Fully charged | Disconnect from power. |
| Off, unit on the cradle | Charging hardware fault | See [charging cradle will not charge the unit](kb-0002.md). |
| Off, unit off the cradle | Unit is powered off | Press and hold the power key for two seconds. |

## Confirming the light is working

If the unit is fully charged and the light is still off, hold the power key for ten
seconds. A healthy unit flashes the light three times during a hard reset. A unit that
never flashes has either a dead battery pack or a failed main board, both of which are
hardware faults.

## What this article cannot tell you

Whether a specific unit is under warranty is not decided here. The returns system checks
