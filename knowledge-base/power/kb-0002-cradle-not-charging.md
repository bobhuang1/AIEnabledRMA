# Charging cradle will not charge the unit

Work through these in order. Each step is cheap and rules out the most common causes
before any part is suspected to be faulty.

## 1. Check the power supply first

The cradle takes a 5 V DC supply rated for at least 1.5 A. A supply that is present but
undersized, or one from a different device family, will power the indicator without
charging the battery. Replace with the specified supply and confirm the light turns solid
amber.

## 2. Clean the charging contacts

Lift the unit out of the cradle and inspect the gold contact pads on the back of the unit
and the pins inside the cradle. Skin oil and lint are the most frequent cause of a cradle
that charges intermittently. Wipe both surfaces with a dry lint-free cloth, then seat the
unit firmly so the light goes solid amber.

## 3. Reseat the unit in the cradle

A unit that sits proud of the cradle contacts will charge intermittently or not at all.
Push the unit down until it seats with a click, and leave it undisturbed for at least ten
minutes. A battery that has been deeply discharged can take that long to show any change
in the light.

## 4. Try a known-good cradle

If the unit charges in a different cradle, the original cradle is faulty. If the unit
does not charge in a different cradle, the fault is in the unit.

## When the fault is in the unit

If the supply is correct, the contacts are clean, the unit seats properly, and a
known-good cradle does not bring the light on, the unit's charging circuit has failed.
Record that all four checks were performed, then continue the return.
